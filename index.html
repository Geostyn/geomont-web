Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

In short: GeoMont does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely offline and declares no Android permissions — not even internet access.

Who is responsible

GeoMont is developed and published by GEOSTYN, based in Belgium.

Contact: geostyn.app@outlook.com

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. We collect no usage statistics and no analytics. GeoMont contains no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device only

So that the app is useful to you, the following is stored locally on your phone:

  • Your preferences: language, theme, unit system and site mode
  • The calculation history you choose to save (maximum 50 entries)
  • Whether your PRO licence is active

This information is kept using Android's local storage. It never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and it is completely removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear the history at any time from the history screen.

Sensors

The Level / Inclinometer module reads your device's accelerometer in order to display the angle in real time. On Android this sensor requires no permission. The readings are processed in memory while the screen is open and are never stored or transmitted.

Sharing results

When you tap "share", the Android system share sheet opens and you choose the destination yourself (WhatsApp, e-mail, notes…). GeoMont only hands the result text to the operating system; it has no access to the app you pick or to what you do afterwards.

In-app purchases

If you buy the PRO version, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. GeoMont never sees, receives or stores payment data such as card numbers or billing addresses. Google only tells the app whether the purchase is active. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

GeoMont is a professional calculation tool. It is not directed at children under 13 and, since it collects no data at all, it processes no information about minors.

Your rights under the GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection regarding your personal data. Because GeoMont neither collects nor retains any personal data, we hold no information about you against which those rights could be exercised. The data stored on your device is under your sole control and you can remove it by clearing the history or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future feature ever requires processing data, we will update this policy and change the "last updated" date before that feature becomes available.

Contact

Any questions about this policy: geostyn.app@outlook.com
